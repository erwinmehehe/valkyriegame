# Word Garden v2 — Literacy Curriculum Foundation

## Purpose

Word Garden is the native literacy world for ValkyrieLearn. Its learning engine should be as deliberate as Math Castle while preserving the stronger physical-adventure structure from the v3.31 reference.

This file defines the first production curriculum foundation. It is **not** a claim of formal alignment with a named national or commercial curriculum. Standards mapping should be reviewed and documented separately before making an alignment claim.

## World structure to preserve

The v3.31 spatial sequence remains the target:

**Flower Gate -> Sunmill Crossing -> Story Hollow**

Learning should unlock or operate the environment rather than appear as floating quiz screens.

Lumi is a functional companion. She should reach high objects, retrieve items, and activate natural or magical mechanisms that Valkyrie cannot reach directly.

## Literacy progression

The production graph now contains these strands:

- phonological awareness
- phonemic awareness
- alphabetic principle
- decoding and encoding
- vocabulary
- comprehension
- storytelling
- Filipino language experiences

The graph is developmental and prerequisite-based. Age is not a ceiling.

### Phonological and phonemic awareness

The sequence begins with listening before print:

- same/different sounds
- rhyme recognition
- syllable counting and blending
- beginning and ending sounds
- oral blending
- phoneme segmentation
- initial-phoneme manipulation as readiness-based stretch

Sound-dependent instruction uses recorded audio. Speech recognition is not required for the first native implementation.

### Alphabetic principle

The child connects sound to print through:

- uppercase letter names
- lowercase letter names
- common consonant sounds
- short vowel sounds
- sound-to-letter mapping

Letter/sound work should use physical stones, flowers, and environmental mechanisms rather than letter-card screens.

### Decoding and encoding

The first decoding path is intentionally compact and teachable:

- blend VC sequences
- blend CVC sequences
- decode simple CVC words
- build CVC words from heard sounds
- transfer a known word pattern to a new word
- recognize early high-frequency words in context

Do not skip oral blending and sound-letter mapping merely because a learner recognizes a few whole words.

### Vocabulary, comprehension, and storytelling

Vocabulary can grow in parallel with decoding.

Activities should include:

- picture-word meaning
- semantic categories
- attributes
- simple context clues
- sentence-to-picture meaning
- event sequencing
- literal story recall
- prediction
- simple inference
- beginning/middle/end retell
- child-created story sequences

Open-ended storytelling is not reduced to right/wrong scoring.

### Filipino experiences

Filipino is modeled as its own language path, not a translation skin over English tasks.

Initial skills include:

- familiar oral vocabulary in context
- syllable awareness
- beginning sounds
- short-story/instruction comprehension

Future content work should use fluent Filipino review for wording, pronunciation, cultural naturalness, and recorded audio.

## Physical mechanic mapping

The curriculum is mapped to reusable Word Garden mechanics:

- **Sound Flowers** — sound matching and phoneme listening
- **Rhyme Vines** — rhyme relationships
- **Syllable Bells** — hear and count syllables
- **Letter Stones** — letter names and sound-letter mapping
- **Seed Blend Path** — combine or segment sound parts
- **Word Blooms** — decoding, word meaning, and pattern transfer
- **Story Lanterns** — sentences, stories, sequencing, prediction, inference
- **Lumi Reach** — companion-assisted retrieval and contextual vocabulary/story actions

The same skill should reappear through more than one representation when possible.

## Hidden placement

The literacy placement blueprint samples progressively harder abilities inside play:

1. sound discrimination
2. syllables
3. beginning sounds
4. consonant sound-letter mapping
5. oral phoneme blending
6. CVC decoding
7. sentence meaning
8. story recall
9. simple inference

Placement is diagnostic only. It may establish provisional readiness, but it must never turn one successful probe into mastery.

## Adaptation rules

Word Garden should reuse the existing learning principles:

- prerequisites control readiness
- one correct answer never equals mastery
- supported success weighs less than independent success
- repeated easy success should deepen the task
- repeated struggle should change representation or scaffold
- secure skills return through spaced review
- mechanic and surface repetition are limited
- world movement and non-academic interactions interrupt long academic runs

## Audio requirement

Recorded instructional audio is required for phoneme-dependent teaching and assessment.

Do not substitute text-to-speech as the only pronunciation model for early phoneme instruction without a separate quality review.
